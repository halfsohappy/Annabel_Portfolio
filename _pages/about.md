    Annabel Lee (she/her) is her.
  <br>
  <br>
A recent graduate of Duke University (‘26), she was the institution’s first Electrical/Computer Engineering & Theater Studies double major, graduating with highest distinction for her thesis project <em>An Accessible Ecosystem for Intuitive Interactions Between Technology and Performer</em>. The project explored how performers could control technical cues for lighting and sound through wearable sensors and earned her a departmental award for excellence in design. Currently she's interested in experimental theater technology such as wearable lighting, indoor positioning systems, and more expressive alternatives to things like "light cues".
<br>
<br>
Her skills include a love and proficiency with just about every tool, from welding stingers, impact drivers, and laser cutters to oscilloscope probes, soldering irons, and sewing machines. She is also a gifted performer and actor with notable experience in puppetry, clown, swing & ballroom dance, as well as over a decade of playing the clarinet.
