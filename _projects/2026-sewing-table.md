<p>
This project may seem a little out of place, but that's okay. I began building out my own textile shop shortly after graduation, inspired by the costume shop in the Duke theater department. One of my favorite features of the shop at Duke were the tables with upholstered tops made of hard foam, so you could easily pin paper and fabric in place for drafting and cutting. I also appreciated the shop's giant designated table for ironing, so in designing this table I made sure to have a substantial section upholstered with ironing board fabric so I would never have to mess with folding ironing boards.
</p>
